# 0001 — Ecological restoration as creative direction

## Context
The project is a low-poly fantasy maze tower defense with competitive play and a future campaign. The founding discussion explored a wetland region inspired by the Lithgow surname.

## Decision proposed
Explore ecological restoration rather than territorial conquest as the foundational creative direction, particularly for the campaign. Use The Lyth and Lythgoe as proposed regional and settlement names.

## Rationale
Defense can protect a recovering ecosystem. Discovery, coexistence and relinquishing control offer a distinctive narrative and progression framework while preserving the existing prototype.

## Alternatives
Conventional tribal warfare; horror-led fantasy; rebuilding the lost civilization unchanged. These remain possible influences but are not the preferred creative emphasis.

## Implications
Explore creatures' ecological roles, restoration-driven progression and temporary defenses. Avoid a simplistic technology-versus-nature binary. No gameplay or roadmap changes are authorized by this record.

## Open questions
Team acceptance; relationship to multiplayer; cause of collapse; magic versus technology; exact geography and victory conditions.
