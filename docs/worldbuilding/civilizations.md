# Civilizations
Playable fantasy tribes remain an option. Contemporary civilizations might rediscover Old Lyth together or independently. Possible ecological philosophies:
- Goblins: adaptation, scavenging and repurposing.
- Freshwater merfolk: circulation and waterways.
- Elves: succession, soil and long-term growth.
- Dwarves: geology, structure and ancient infrastructure.
None is inherently good or bad. These are speculative concepts, not replacements for the tribes listed in [existing design](../design.md). Player identity, faction roster and cooperation remain unresolved.
