# Instructions for `docs/worldbuilding/`

Rules for the world's lore. [`docs/AGENTS.md`](../AGENTS.md) (statuses, decisions) still applies.

## Before you write

- Read the [index](README.md) and the [vision](../vision.md), then only the pages the task needs.
- Check [decisions](../decisions/README.md) to see what has actually been agreed.

## Rules

- **Never quietly turn speculation into canon.** Only a page or decision with `status: accepted` is canon.
- Say who an idea came from. Keep ideas a person endorsed apart from possibilities an agent suggested.
- Put new raw ideas in [`ideas/`](ideas/README.md) and link them from its index. Move them into a topic page (geography, ecology…) only once they're developed.
- Leave the mysteries the team chose to keep open. Don't fill them in.
- Not every piece of lore needs a game mechanic. Don't force one.
