# Ecology
Old Lyth is alive but dysfunctional. The campaign could restore ecological relationships and resilience rather than conquer territory.
Explore mutualism, succession, trophic cascades, migration, decomposition and watershed dynamics. Disturbed creatures might become beneficial when conditions change. Restoration need not mean a static equilibrium or a return to an idealized past.
A possible loop: discover, stabilize, restore, rewild. Defenses protect recovery and may eventually become unnecessary. These are campaign hypotheses, not implemented mechanics. See [ideas](ideas/founding-discussion.md).
