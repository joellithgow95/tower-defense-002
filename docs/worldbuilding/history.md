# History
Contemporary civilizations rediscover Old Lyth and investigate Lythgoe, potentially its only recognizable surviving building or settlement. The ecosystem has undergone severe stress and its inhabitants no longer coexist as before.
One hypothesis: an ancient civilization regulated water, growth and animal populations until natural feedback loops weakened; abandoned infrastructure may still operate after its collapse. This is not established history. The collapse, inhabitants, chronology and magic/technology relationship remain open. Restoration might involve dismantling or repurposing artifacts rather than rebuilding the old civilization.
