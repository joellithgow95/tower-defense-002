# Founding discussion
## Origins
The family surname Lithgow inspired the landscape through its reported place-name meaning, a damp hollow by a lake. The separate gray-dog folk etymology inspired Gray Dog Games and possible heraldry. The user preferred The Lyth as region and Lythgoe as town, and endorsed ecological restoration as the creative north star. Historical etymology has not been independently verified.
## Possibilities (not canon)
- Names: Graymere, Blackfen, The Sinks, Hound's Rest, Upper Lyth.
- Gray hounds as scouts, guardians or heraldic symbols.
- Causeways, rising water, fog, soft peat and elevated ground as possible level constraints.
- Ancient machinery regulating growth or water; its removal could restore feedback loops.
- Creature concepts: Mireback (soil aeration), Reedling (bank stabilization), Lantern Moth (pollination), Fen Hound (predation), Stonecrawler (mineral breakdown), Sporekin (decomposition). Disturbed behaviors could become healthy ecological roles.
- Former enemies might become allies through habitat recovery; abandoned towers could become nesting sites.
- Campaign loop: discover, stabilize, restore, rewild; restoration could reduce reliance on defenses.
## Open questions
How does campaign restoration coexist with competitive maze defense? Is there a single protagonist or faction selection? What happened to Old Lyth? What does restoration mean to different civilizations? How much technology should remain? What is Lythgoe physically?
