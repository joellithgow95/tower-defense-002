# Second worldbuilding discussion
## Earlier historical sketches retained for reference
Five ages: Living Lyth, Age of Kinship, Great Ordering, Long Silence, Returning. Possible infrastructure: waterworks, growing houses, calling stones, seasonal engines. Possible collapse accounts: departure, cascading unraveling, transformation. These were brainstorms; the collaborator was not sold on a rise/fall or technology-bad framing. Do not treat this sequence as history.
## Character sketches
Human steward concerned with food; human naturalist advocating learning from neighbors; wetland elder distrustful of concessions; goblin engineer interested in reopening infrastructure. Each faction has internal disagreements. Species and allegiance need not coincide.
## References
Pillars of Eternity II: Deadfire and Fallout: New Vegas (factions and contested territory); Tactics Ogre (tactical politics); Rain World (independent ecology); Dishonored (discoverable alternatives).
## Visual direction
Exploratory moodboards combined folkloric naturalism, pastoral storybook warmth and low-poly miniature dioramas. Mossy ruins, monumental waterworks, wetland settlements and inhabited biomes were especially promising. Generated images are not included in this PR.
## Next exploration
Regional geography and inhabitants together; material culture; opening chapter; mission design. Keep external-world lore secondary.
