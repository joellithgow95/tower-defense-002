# Wisp and Mire visual language

Joel approved the W1 Lantern Wanderer and M1 Walking Bog concepts for model exploration, with faceless Wisp, organic Mire and restrained detail. This is art direction, not accepted lore or gameplay rules.

- **Wisp (`wisp`):** vertical, tapered ivory and sage mist folds around exposed player-coloured light. No face or limbs. Cursor is a slender faceted light with two trailing folds. Possible future motifs: pointed canopies, suspended lanterns, open frames and reeds.
- **Mire (`mire`):** broad moss and peat layers, four short root-like feet, a circumferential coloured fissure and a small exposed light atop the moss. Cursor is a broad light with suspended earth fragments. Possible future motifs: terraced foundations, mossy roofs and embedded light.

Both retain the existing player palette. Identity comes from geometry rather than ownership colour. Light given form and landscape given life are visual metaphors, not an assertion about the spirits' origins. Future transformation motion and architecture remain exploratory; no new mechanics are implied.
