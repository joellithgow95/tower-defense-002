# Lythgoe
A town or ancient remnant in the Lyth, potentially the only recognizable surviving structure of Old Lyth. Its secrets may drive campaign discovery.
A possible campaign hub evolves from abandoned monumental architecture toward an integrated living landscape. Buildings may be preserved, repurposed or dismantled. Bringing Lythgoe back to life need not mean rebuilding its former civilization.
Exact scale, original function, inhabitants and campaign role remain open.
