# Geography
The Lyth is a region, not necessarily a kingdom. Old Lyth is a rediscovered part of it. Lythgoe is a named settlement or remnant.
Explore lochs, peat, moss, reeds, flooded forests, mist, raised settlements and causeways. The watershed could define regional identity rather than political borders. The landscape should feel alive, beautiful, melancholy and sometimes frightening, not primarily horrific.
Boundaries, climate and map remain open. See [ideas](ideas/founding-discussion.md).
