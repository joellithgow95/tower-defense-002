# Creative vision
> A tower defense game about ecological restoration rather than territorial conquest.
>
> Defense becomes a means of protecting a recovering ecosystem, rather than simply defeating an invading army.
>
> The final victory isn't a stronger fortress. It's a world that no longer needs one.

## Pillars
- Fantasy rooted in wetland landscapes, folklore, curiosity and wonder rather than horror.
- Creatures are participants in ecosystems, not simply enemies.
- Explore the distinction between participating in nature and controlling it; technology is not inherently harmful.
- Rediscovery and restoration can shape campaign progression without dictating competitive modes.

## Scope
This direction was endorsed in the founding discussion by one collaborator; team acceptance remains pending. Existing maze defense, multiplayer and puzzle plans remain intact. Old Lyth is a working title, not a finalized game name. Gray Dog Games is an enthusiastically received studio-name proposal inspired by the surname's gray-dog folk etymology.
