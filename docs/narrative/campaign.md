# Campaign foundations
## Focus
Discover the Lyth from within. External civilizations can remain undefined. The mystery is local, not an obligation to explain the entire world.
## Opening possibility
A human or apparently human protagonist wakes near the lake with little memory. A struggling human settlement offers shelter and a sense of belonging. Its accounts of dangerous creatures and ancestral land shape the player's first allegiance through gratitude and familiarity, not necessarily deliberate deception.
## Development
Encounters with other communities reveal that human history is not the whole history of the Lyth. Some old inhabitants never left. The protagonist can help human neighbors find a viable place in the region without taking over other homes. Relationships with individuals may diverge from faction reputation; internal political movements may respond differently.
## Mystery
The protagonist might never have left the Lyth; unfamiliar languages, recognition by inhabitants or responses from old mechanisms could hint at a past. Avoid committing to chosen-one or reincarnated-king explanations. Identity may emerge through relationships rather than ancestry.
## Open questions
Amnesia is optional. Lythgoe's role, opening location, protagonist origin, faction structure and ending remain unresolved.
